# Academic language and LaTeX safety

## Language policy

- Manuscript process and article prose guidance: British English.
- Slides may intentionally remain in Portuguese; preserve that scope when requested.

## Rules

1. Do not break LaTeX commands or environments (`\cite`, `\ref`, `\begin{...}`, etc.).
2. Prefer precise, concise, formal academic phrasing.
3. Use minimal diffs unless a broader rewrite is explicitly requested.
4. Separate language polishing from factual correction when possible.

## Scope

- Article chapters in `content/`.
- Supporting text in `main.tex` and `apresentacoes/` where applicable.
